02 / Foundations

Ink, paper
and a little colour.

Neutral surfaces do the heavy lifting. Three stable accents give structure and make relationships easy to follow.

Reading hierarchy

Space Grotesk

Useful.
Not ordinary.

Regular-weight headings with close tracking and confident scale. Avoid heavy, oversized marketing type everywhere.

Reading rhythm

DM Sans

Clear language.
Comfortable reading.
Small details that belong.

Use 14-16px body text, generous line height, concise labels and visible hierarchy. Small map labels are not body-text defaults.

Regular 400Medium 500Semibold 600
A

Colour has a job.

Choose domain-specific categories. Do not use the three route colours as an arbitrary rainbow.

B

Paper carries content.

Warm panels, narrow rules, square corners and deliberate spacing. Reserve rounded geometry for the dock and nodes.

C

Structure stays legible.

Horizontal labels, stable alignment and clear destinations. Text and selected shapes reinforce colour.

04 / Adaptation rules

Borrow the language.
Not the whole story.

A different project needs its own information architecture, identity and task flows.

+ Keep the character
  • Ink canvas and warm reading surfaces
  • Space Grotesk and DM Sans
  • Stable category colour and square badges
  • Fine rules, factual rows and clear hierarchy
  • Purposeful, restrained transitions
↗ Change the context
  • Brand, content and category meanings
  • Navigation to suit the actual workflow
  • Map only if relationships need explaining
  • No career content or skill passengers
  • No trains required to make the style work

Small screens

Reflow, do not shrink.

Stack content below 760px. Keep readable labels and practical touch targets. Replace a cramped diagram with a list or simpler navigation.

Inclusive by default

Useful without motion.

Keep keyboard focus visible. Pair colour with labels. Provide a text equivalent for diagrams, and preserve reading at 200% text zoom.
